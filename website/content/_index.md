title = "Flawrence"
+++
Flawrence maps a codebase's attack surface and assigns research agents to individual entry points. Candidate findings are independently checked before submission.

- Use Anthropic, OpenAI, Workers AI or compatible endpoints.
- Configure agents through Markdown.
- Run locally or in an isolated container.

> [!WARNING]
> Agents execute code. Use an isolated environment and configure a cost limit.
